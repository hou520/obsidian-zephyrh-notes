al_semantic 默认意图——闲聊意图描述内容调整为：
该意图仅适用于玩家与客服之间的纯社交对话，内容包括一般问候、感谢、祝福、表达情绪或无具体目的的轻松交谈。若玩家的消息涉及游戏内的任何元素（如功能、机制、活动、团队协作、策略、聊天频道等），则不应识别为闲聊。尤其当消息描述的是玩家与其他玩家之间的沟通、协作、或询问游戏中是否存在某种聊天/交互功能时，即使使用了'chat'、'talk'等词，也不属于闲聊范畴，而应归入其他相关意图。

This intent applies only to purely social conversations between players and customer service, including general greetings, thanks, well wishes, expressions of emotion, or lighthearted chat without a specific purpose. If a player's message relates to any element of the game (such as features, mechanics, events, teamwork, strategies, chat channels, etc.), it should not be considered casual chat. In particular, when a message describes communication or collaboration between players, or inquires about the existence of a certain chat/interaction feature in the game, even if words like 'chat' or 'talk' are used, it does not fall under the category of casual chat but should be classified as other relevant intents.
